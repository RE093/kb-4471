How the rows are counted

The tenant decides the cause, not the intake tag: intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and its tags disagree with the cause in both directions (the “tagged as” column). A report the desk read as a one-day move is counted there, whatever its tenant. The export repeats some reports across overlapping pages; each id counts once.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone, and every row above has the same kind of subject: “wrong day”, “date is wrong”. Matching subjects say two reports sound alike, not that they share a cause. Its consequence, “engineering merges them later”, ran the other way here: different causes ended up in one ticket and never came apart.

Lessons

  • Before marking a big ticket known, check that one fix closes every report in it.
  • Count by cause, not by subject or intake tag.
